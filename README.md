# Pathly

**Your future. Your pathway. Your progress.**

Pathly is a proposed mobile-first learning and future-pathways platform for Victorian VCE and VCE Vocational Major (VCE VM) students.

It brings together structured learning, AI-assisted study support, practice, progress tracking and post-school pathway exploration.

> **Project status:** Early planning and product design. This repository contains project documentation, not a finished or deployable application. Described features are proposals unless explicitly marked as implemented.

## Product pillars

- **Learn:** structured subject learning, explanations, practice and assessment preparation.
- **Plan:** explore university, TAFE, apprenticeships, employment and alternative entry routes.
- **Progress:** set goals, track learning, identify gaps and recognise improvement.

Pathly is designed for both VCE and VCE VM. It should not assume every student is pursuing an ATAR or university.

## Principles

- Support VCE and VCE VM as distinct pathways.
- Teach understanding rather than doing students' assessed work for them.
- Do not present AI feedback as official VCAA marks or admissions advice.
- Use original, licensed or permissioned learning content.
- Minimise student data collection and protect student privacy.
- Verify and date-stamp changing course and pathway information.
- Build a useful mobile-first web experience before considering native apps.

# Validation Plan

## Core question
Do VCE and VCE VM students want a connected product for learning support, practice, progress and post-school pathway exploration?

## Discovery target
Aim for approximately 20–30 student conversations with representation from both VCE and VCE VM. This is a practical discovery goal, not a statistically representative sample.

## Interview questions
1. What is hardest about studying or managing school right now?
2. How do you prepare for SACs, assessments or exams?
3. What do you do when a topic does not make sense?
4. Which tools and resources do you already use?
5. What frustrates you about them?
6. How confident are you about post-school options?
7. How do you find prerequisites and alternative pathways?
8. Which part of the Pathly concept would be most useful?
9. What would make you stop using it?
10. Would you pay for any part? What would need to be included?

Avoid leading questions that pressure students to praise the idea.

## Prototype tasks
Ask students to find a topic, complete a lesson, ask for an explanation, answer a practice question and locate an official course requirement. Observe hesitation and confusion.

## Evidence
Record repeated pain points, differences between VCE and VM needs, unprompted feature use, trust concerns, willingness to return and willingness to pay.

## Decision rule
Narrow the product if the full concept is too broad. Prioritise repeated student problems over features that are merely exciting to build.

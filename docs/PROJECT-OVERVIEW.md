# Project Overview

## Vision
Pathly connects secondary learning, study support, progress and post-school planning for Victorian VCE and VCE VM students.

**Promise:** Help students understand where they want to go, what they need to achieve and how to get there.

## The three pillars
- **Learn:** structured learning paths, explanations, practice and assessment preparation.
- **Plan:** university, TAFE, apprenticeship, employment and alternative-entry exploration.
- **Progress:** goals, learning milestones, topic confidence and useful next actions.

## Audience
- VCE students across relevant year levels.
- VCE VM students working across Literacy, Numeracy, Work Related Skills, Personal Development Skills and VET.
- Students who are undecided about their post-school direction.

VCE VM must have a distinct experience. It must not be treated as a reduced VCE pathway or assumed to be ATAR/university-focused.

## Product principles
1. Support different definitions of success.
2. Make AI a learning aid, not a substitute for student work.
3. Explain uncertainty and use authoritative sources.
4. Keep motivation positive and non-punitive.
5. Design mobile-first.
6. Minimise and protect student data, especially because users may be minors.

## Validation
The central product assumptions must be tested with students and educators. Interview a mix of VCE and VCE VM students, prototype core tasks and observe whether the integrated experience solves a repeated problem.

## Status
This repository documents a concept and proposed implementation. It is not a completed application, verified curriculum database or live AI integration.

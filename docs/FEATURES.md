# Feature Catalogue

*Proposed features; not all are implemented.*

## Home dashboard
- Pathway and year-level overview.
- Continue-learning action.
- Weekly goal and progress.
- Student-entered upcoming tasks.
- Recommended next actions and achievements.

## My Learning
- Subject/learning-area overview.
- Units, topics and short lessons.
- Worked examples and knowledge checks.
- Practice activities and topic mastery.
- Revision and optional diagnostic checks.

## AI Study Assistant
- Subject-aware explanations.
- Simpler explanations and alternate examples.
- Hints and worked examples.
- Generated practice questions.
- Revision planning and feedback on practice responses.
- Clear limitations and sources where available.

## Practice Centre
- Multiple-choice and short-answer activities.
- Topic filters and mixed revision.
- Explanations, retries and saved questions.
- Performance summaries.

## My Pathway
- University and TAFE exploration.
- Apprenticeship and employment information.
- Prerequisites and selection requirements.
- Alternative entry routes where officially documented.
- Shortlists and next-step checklists.

## Resources
- Original revision guides.
- Assessment preparation advice.
- Official external resources.
- Filters by pathway, subject and topic.

## Motivation
- XP for meaningful learning.
- Topic milestones and badges.
- Optional weekly goals and streaks.
- No shaming or penalties for missed days.

## Later possibilities
Expanded subjects, full practice exams, deeper analytics, school licences, moderated peer study and other Australian curricula.

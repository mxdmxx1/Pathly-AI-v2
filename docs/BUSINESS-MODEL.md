# Business Model

## Initial hypothesis: freemium

### Free tier (proposed)
- Basic dashboard and progress.
- Selected learning paths.
- Limited AI study assistance.
- Selected practice and resources.
- Basic pathway exploration.

### Premium tier (hypothesis)
Potential value includes expanded AI usage, fuller learning paths, a larger practice library, more detailed feedback and advanced revision/progress insights.

## Pricing to test
Previously considered hypotheses:
- A$8.99 per month.
- A$69.99 per year.

These are not validated prices or revenue forecasts.

## Cost and validation
Measure AI cost per active student, impose fair-use limits, monitor hosting/payment costs and validate willingness to pay through interviews and prototype testing. Do not promise unlimited costly AI access before unit economics are understood.

School or tutoring-provider licences may be explored later, after student value is demonstrated.

## Founder considerations
Payment providers, business accounts and contracts may have age-related eligibility requirements. As the founder is under 18, seek appropriate adult and professional guidance before accepting payments or entering binding agreements. This is not legal, tax or financial advice.

# Product Requirements

## Onboarding
- Select VCE or VCE VM and year level.
- Select subjects or learning areas.
- Optionally add interests, goals and challenging topics.
- Allow "I'm still exploring" for future plans.
- Explain data collection and provide sensible skip options.

## Dashboard
- Show learning progress and a manageable weekly goal.
- Provide a continue-learning shortcut.
- Surface student-entered upcoming tasks.
- Recommend a small number of relevant next actions.

## Learning paths
- Organise content by pathway, subject/learning area, unit, topic and lesson.
- Include explanations, examples, knowledge checks and practice.
- Allow revision and diagnostic checks.
- Identify curriculum version and source where relevant.

## AI study assistant
- Use pathway, subject and topic context.
- Explain concepts, offer hints and generate practice.
- Help students understand feedback and plan revision.
- Encourage reasoning instead of simply supplying answers.
- Clearly state that AI feedback is not official VCAA marking.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and link to authoritative sources for high-stakes information.
- Apply age-appropriate safety and privacy controls.

## Practice centre
- Topic and mixed practice.
- Explanations after attempts.
- Retry and review options.
- Clearly label generated or unofficial questions.

## Pathway explorer
- Cover university, TAFE, apprenticeships, employment and alternative entry.
- Show requirements only when verified.
- Link to official provider/admissions sources.
- Store source URL, applicable year and last-checked date.
- Present multiple possible routes, not a single prescribed outcome.

## Progress and resources
- Track lesson completion, practice and self-reported confidence.
- Offer actionable strengths/gaps summaries.
- Use original, licensed or permissioned resources.

## Non-functional requirements
- Responsive, accessible mobile-first interface.
- Secure authentication and per-user data access controls.
- Server-side API secrets only.
- Minimal collection and retention of personal data.
- Rate limits, error handling, monitoring and cost controls.

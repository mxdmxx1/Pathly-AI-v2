# Competitive Positioning

## Positioning hypothesis
Pathly connects learning and assessment support, personal progress and post-school pathway exploration. The differentiator should be the connected student journey, not simply an AI chatbot or gamified lessons.

## Potential differentiators
- Designed for Victorian VCE and VCE VM contexts.
- Distinct applied-learning and VET support.
- Curriculum-aware AI that teaches concepts.
- Learning progress connected to practical next steps.
- Exploration across university, TAFE, apprenticeships and employment.
- Official sources for changing admissions and prerequisite information.

## Risks
- Students already have free resources and general-purpose AI tools.
- Existing platforms may offer strong tutoring or practice.
- Accurate curriculum content takes time.
- Admissions information changes.
- Broad scope may exceed a small team's capacity.

## Research required
Compare current products, pricing, subject coverage, VM support and user reviews. Interview students about the tools they actually use before making public competitor claims.

## Positioning test
After seeing a prototype, ask students to explain Pathly in their own words. If they describe it only as another AI homework chatbot, the differentiation is not clear enough.

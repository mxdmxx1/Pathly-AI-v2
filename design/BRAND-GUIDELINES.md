# Brand Guidelines

## Name
**Pathly** is the working product name. Trademark, business-name and domain availability have not been checked.

## Tagline
**Your future. Your pathway. Your progress.**

## Personality
Modern, clear, encouraging, trustworthy and ambitious without being intimidating. Professional rather than childish.

## Palette

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Deep navy | `#142B3B` |
| Green accent | `#16A36A` |
| Off-white background | `#F5F7F8` |
| White surface | `#FFFFFF` |
| Slate secondary text | `#718096` |
| Light green accent | `#E5F5ED` |

Maintain accessible contrast and never rely on colour alone to communicate status.

## Logo direction
Preferred concept: a pathway-inspired "P" representing direction, movement and progress.

Maintain a primary symbol-plus-wordmark lockup and a symbol-only app icon. The current direction is conceptual and should be refined into vector artwork before launch.

## Interface
Use a mobile-first layout, clear hierarchy, restrained cards, readable content blocks and consistent navigation. Gamification should motivate without feeling childish or pressuring.

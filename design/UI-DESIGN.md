# UI Design Direction

## Goals
Make it obvious where a student is, what they can do next, how learning is progressing and where future-pathway information lives.

## Proposed navigation
1. Home
2. Learn
3. AI Tutor
4. Practice
5. Pathway

Resources can be linked from Home and relevant learning screens.

## Core screens
- **Onboarding:** short pathway, year, subject and optional interest setup.
- **Home:** continue learning, weekly goal, progress and a few recommendations.
- **Learn:** subject overview, units, topics, lessons and practice.
- **AI Tutor:** subject context, suggested prompts, limitations and sources.
- **Practice:** topic filters, question interaction, feedback and retries.
- **Pathway:** search and compare options with requirements and official links.
- **Resources:** curated original, licensed and official external resources.

## Interaction principles
- Give feedback after meaningful actions.
- Make progress visible but not overwhelming.
- Keep key actions reachable on mobile.
- Support keyboard navigation and accessible labels.
- Include empty, loading and error states.
- Clearly label demo data and simulated functionality in prototypes.
